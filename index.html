Projects

orbtrisk-cockpit

Python · FastAPI · SGP4 · astropy

Orbital conjunction screening and triage. It propagates TLEs with SGP4, converts TEME states to GCRS, flags close approaches within a configurable volume (default 10 km over 14 days), parses CCSDS CDM (KVN) messages, and groups repeat alerts into one event with an update history. Each event gets a Low/Watch/High tier and an A–D confidence grade, and operator decisions go into an audit log.

Scope: screening only. No probability of collision and no orbit determination. 21 automated tests (pytest).
